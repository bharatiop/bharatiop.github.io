\cvsection{Research Profile}
Nuclear astrophysicist working at the interface of nuclear structure and
compact-star astrophysics. My group builds relativistic mean-field
energy-density functionals --- the IOPB-I, G3, NITR and NITR-I interactions ---
that are calibrated to finite nuclei and then pushed to the densities inside
neutron stars, so that terrestrial measurements such as PREX and CREX can be
confronted directly with gravitational-wave, NICER and pulsar-timing
observations. Current work covers dark-matter-admixed and anisotropic compact
objects, exotic core composition (hyperons, $\Delta$ baryons, quarkyonic and
quark matter), radial and non-radial oscillation modes, and Bayesian and
nonparametric inference of the dense-matter equation of state.
